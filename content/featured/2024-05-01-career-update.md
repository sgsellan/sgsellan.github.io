I am beyond excited to announce that I will be joining the Computer Science Department at Columbia University as a tenure-track Assistant Professor. My appointment will begin on July 1st, 2025; until then, I will be working as a postdoctoral associate with Prof. Justin Solomon at MIT.

This piece of news is nothing short of a dream come true for me, and it would not be possible without the help of many mentors that I am eternally grateful to; most of all, my PhD advisor Prof. Alec Jacobson. As much as this moment feels like a culmination of the work of many years, it also marks the start of an equal-parts-exciting-equal-parts-terrifying stage in my life and career. Stay tuned to this site for updates!

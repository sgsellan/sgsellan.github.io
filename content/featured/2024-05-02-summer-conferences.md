While I have been pretty busy over the past couple of months, I am very lucky to have incredibly smart, hard-working collaborators, that will give me an excuse to attend a bunch of conferences this summer. Hit me up if you'll be in any of these and want to chat!

- I will be at [CVPR 2024](https://cvpr.thecvf.com) in Seattle, Washington, thanks to the brilliant student [Lily Goli](https://lilygoli.github.io), who led [this project](https://bayesrays.github.io) on quantifying the uncertainty in learned Neural Radiance Fields.
- I will be at [SGP 2024](https://sgp2024.github.io) in Cambridge, Massachusetts, between July 22-26, where I have served as the [Graduate School Chair](https://sgp2024.github.io/program/) for the conference together with [Ed Chien](https://cs-people.bu.edu/edchien/).
- I will be at [SIGGRAPH 2024](https://s2024.siggraph.org) in Denver, Colorado thanks to [Yuta Noma](https://yutanoma.com)'s work on [surface-filling curves](https://www.dgp.toronto.edu/projects/surface-filling-curves/) and my collaboration with [Oded Stein](https://odedstein.com), [Samara Ren](https://samararen.github.io) and [Christopher Batty](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~c2batty/) on surface reconstruction from Signed Distance Functions.

Can't wait to see you all!

Meet Me at This Summer's Conferences!

While I have been pretty busy over the past couple of months, I am very lucky to have incredibly smart, hard-working collaborators, that will give me an excuse to attend a bunch of conferences this summer. Hit me up if you’ll be in any of these and want to chat!

  • I will be at CVPR 2024 in Seattle, Washington, thanks to the brilliant student Lily Goli, who led this project on quantifying the uncertainty in learned Neural Radiance Fields.
  • I will be at SGP 2024 in Cambridge, Massachusetts, where I have served as the Graduate School Chair for the conference together with Ed Chien.
  • I will be at SIGGRAPH 2024 in Denver, Colorado thanks to Yuta Noma’s work on surface-filling curves and my collaboration with Oded Stein, Samara Ren and Christopher Batty on surface reconstruction from Signed Distance Functions.

Can’t wait to see you all!
